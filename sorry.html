A little something for you

Dear Siddhi,

Some things are easier to express
through a little gesture.

♡

I have something for you.

Made with a little love and sincerity.
Just for you, Siddhi

I'm Sorry.

💐
✿ ✿ ✿
I know flowers can't undo
everything, and neither can
a simple apology.

But I genuinely want you to know
that I'm sorry if I hurt you.
That was never what I wanted.

You deserve kindness, happiness,
and all the beautiful things
this world has to offer.

So here's a little bouquet
to brighten your day. 🌷
With a sincere apology ♡

Whatever happens,
I hope you have a beautiful day. ♡

A little page, just for Siddhi.
